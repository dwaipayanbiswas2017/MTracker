Getting started

You can be tracking in a few minutes. Follow these steps once, then keep adding entries as money moves.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register with your name, email and a password. If email verification is enabled, enter the one-time code (OTP) sent to your inbox.

  2. 2

    Create your first month

    On the dashboard, open the month selector and create the month you want to track. You can optionally copy pending items from the previous month.

  3. 3

    Set opening balances

    For each account (for example Cash, SBI-2390), enter how much money it holds at the start of the month. This is your “available from previous month”.

  4. 4

    Add income and spending

    Record income, paid expenses, daily spends, pending bills and long-term debts. Everything is saved automatically as you add, edit or delete entries.

Tip: Set your default account and currency in Profile settings so new entries are pre-filled.

Dashboard & totals

The dashboard is built around one month at a time. Each number answers a different question, so here is what they mean.

Available

Opening balances plus all income recorded for the month.

Expenses

Paid expenses plus daily expenses — everything you have actually spent.

Current balance

Available minus what you have already spent — the money you have right now.

Expected balance

Current balance minus pending bills — what should be left after planned payments.

The per-account panel shows the running balance for each account based on its opening balance, income and expenses.

Money features

MTracker separates money into a few clear buckets so reports stay meaningful.

Income

Salary, refunds, freelance payments or any money coming in. Each entry has an amount, source, account and date, and increases that account's balance.

Paid expenses

Payments you have already made — rent, bills, groceries and so on. They reduce the balance of the account you paid from.

Daily expenses

Small, frequent personal spends. They are tracked separately for quick review but are still included in your total expenses and balances.

Pending / budget

Bills you expect but have not paid yet. They do not reduce your current balance, but they are subtracted when calculating the expected balance. When a month is created you can copy the previous month's pending items.

Long pending / debts

Money you owe or that is owed to you over a longer period. Record partial payments as you pay them down; each payment creates a linked expense so your records stay in sync.

Transfers between accounts

Moving money from one account to another creates a linked expense and income pair, so both account balances stay accurate without changing your overall net position.

Notes

Jot down reminders or context for the month — for example a bill due date or a savings goal.

Accounts & categories

Accounts represent where money lives; categories describe what money was for.

Accounts — add banks, wallets or cash. Set an opening balance per account for each month and mark one as your default. Renaming an account updates it everywhere.

Categories — group spending (for example Food, Transport, Bills). They make the expense list easier to scan and keep reports consistent.

Months

Each month is a self-contained snapshot. Create a new month when the calendar turns, optionally copying pending items so recurring bills are not forgotten. Deleting a month removes its transactions and cannot be undone, so export or back up first if you need a copy.

Privacy & PIN lock

Money is personal. MTracker includes an optional PIN that hides sensitive amounts when you are in a shared space.

  • Set a PIN from the dashboard; amounts stay hidden until you unlock them.
  • Entering the correct PIN unlocks the figures immediately — no extra button press.
  • Wrong PINs clear the field and show an error so you can retry safely.
  • Your PIN can be changed from the dashboard at any time.

AI assistant & MCP

MTracker ships two ways to work with your data using natural language.

Built-in AI assistant

Open Chat from the dashboard. Ask questions like “how much did I spend on food this month?” or “add a 500 expense for petrol”, and the assistant uses the same tools as the web UI. It always acts with your permissions.

MCP server

Connect Claude, Gemini or any MCP-compatible client to your account. {% if current_user.is_authenticated %} Create a personal access token on the API Keys page, then follow the MCP Help guide for setup and the full tool reference. {% else %} Sign in to create a personal access token and read the MCP setup guide with the full tool reference. {% endif %}

Read-only tokens are available — perfect for giving an assistant visibility without permission to change anything.

CSV import

Already tracking in a spreadsheet? Import a CSV with sections for paid expenses, pending items, personal expenses, monthly income and opening balances. The importer maps each section into the matching bucket, so review the column names before uploading. The AI assistant and MCP clients can also import pasted CSV text.

Data & security

  • Your data is private to your account; sessions are protected with HTTP-only cookies.
  • Administrators can schedule automatic backups. Ask your instance owner if you need a copy of your data.
  • Use a strong, unique password and keep your PIN and API tokens private. Revoke tokens you no longer use from the API Keys page.
  • Always sign out on shared devices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to read this page?

No. This help centre is public. You only need an account to store and manage your own financial data.

What is the difference between pending and long pending?

Pending items are short-term bills expected in this month. Long pending covers debts or obligations that span months, with support for partial payments.

Will daily expenses be counted in my totals?

Yes. They are shown separately for convenience but are included in total expenses and account balances.

Is my data saved automatically?

Yes — adding, editing or deleting an entry saves the month to the database automatically.

Can I use MTracker from my phone?

Yes. The dashboard and this help page are responsive, with touch-friendly controls and a light/dark theme.
